# Wine Guide — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

## The short version

Wine Guide works entirely on your device. We do not run analytics, we do not
show ads, and there is no account to create.

## What the app stores

Your cellar, tasting notes, drink-by reminders, quiz answers, and fridge
selections are stored **on your device only** (AsyncStorage). Uninstalling the
app deletes them. Nothing is uploaded to us — we have no servers for this data.

## Purchases (Wine Guide Pro)

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and processed by **RevenueCat**
(`react-native-purchases`). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and
your purchase history so we can unlock Pro features. It is not linked to your
name, email, or any account. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Notifications

Drink-by reminders are **local notifications** scheduled on your device. No
push server is used; nothing leaves the phone.

## Third-party content

Wine reference content (bottle info, regions, styles) is fetched from the
public VinoFYI encyclopedia API. These requests carry no personal data.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No account or sign-in
- No sale or sharing of personal information (there is none to share)
